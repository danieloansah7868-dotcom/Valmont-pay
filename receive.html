How direct settlement works

1
You create a Pay Me link and choose the entitled account — your MoMo, your bank, or any ACCT_… subaccount.
2
Payer opens link, enters amount (if you left it open) + email, pays via MoMo / Card / Bank Transfer through Paystack.
3
Settlement:
  • Subaccount (ACCT_…): Paystack splits at source — your share settles to that account’s bank instantly.
  • Bank freeform: Paystack settles T+1 to the main settlement bank (or you add that bank as a subaccount).
  • MoMo number: On charge.success webhook, Valmont triggers a Paystack Transfer (recipients API) → instant MoMo credit. Needs Paystack balance + transfer enabled.

Valmont Pay never holds money

Same as your dashboard: “Funds settle directly to your bank via Paystack.” This page just lets the receiver name the destination per link, instead of one global settlement account.

Which destination should you use?

📱 MoMo numberBest for P2P / freelancers. Instant payout via Transfer API.
🏦 Bank accountName it freeform for record; for real split use ACCT_.
ACCT_… subaccountTrue Paystack split — merchant’s own ledger & settlement.

Create a Paystack subaccount in Dashboard → Paystack → Subaccounts, then paste ACCT_… here. That merchant sees the ledger under their tenant.
