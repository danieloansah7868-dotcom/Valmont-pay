Key Features

Locked Amounts

Every link carries a one-time access code. The price is resolved server-side — a customer cannot change GH₵1,400 to GH₵1 by editing the URL. No surprises on either side.

Web projects don't ship in one go. Send a Stage 1 link, a Stage 2 link, and a handover link — each with its own locked amount and reference. Your ledger stays clean.
