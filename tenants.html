Tenant Management

Tenants are registered merchants on the Valmont-Pay gateway. Each tenant gets their own API keys, can receive signed webhooks when payments succeed, and can be linked from pay.html?tenant=<tenant_id> or checkout.html?tenant=<tenant_id>. Webhooks are signed with an HMAC-SHA512 secret per tenant — tenants verify the x-valmontpay-signature header to confirm the POST came from Valmont-Pay.
